## Typefaces

The page embeds three typefaces as base64 `woff2` data URIs. All three are
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose full text is in
[`fonts/OFL.txt`](fonts/OFL.txt).

| Typeface | Copyright | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Barlow | Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors — https://github.com/jpt/barlow | Body text, answers, UI |
| Barlow Condensed | Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors — https://github.com/jpt/barlow | Headings, HUD, scores |
| Overpass | Copyright Red Hat, Inc. — https://github.com/RedHatOfficial/Overpass | The faces of the road signs |

Overpass is a Highway Gothic derivative, which is why the signs' lettering looks
like real Ontario signage.

## Source material

Content is written from **The Official MTO Driver's Handbook** (ISBN
978-1-4868-8628-9), chapters 1 and 2, and the traffic signs, lights and pavement
markings pages on ontario.ca. The handbook is Crown copyright, King's Printer for
Ontario. Nothing here reproduces its text: the questions, explanations and study

scratch as SVG rather than copied artwork.

This is an unofficial study aid with no connection to the Ministry of
Transportation or DriveTest. Always confirm current rules at
[ontario.ca](https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook).
